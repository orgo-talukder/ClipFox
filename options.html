YouTube Link Extractor

v1.4.0

Settings

Find the links. Clip the clutter. Choose how Clipfox copies links, watches player changes, and reports counts in your toolbar.

Copy format

Used by Copy all, Copy N, and Export. Per-card copy always copies that card's URL.

Output

Plain URLs, one per line. Video IDs mode skips playlists and channels.

Watching

Watch mode checks the embedded player every few seconds while the popup is open and refreshes the results when the player changes.

Live

Toolbar badge

The badge shows how many YouTube links were found on the last scanned tab. It clears when that tab navigates or closes.

Icon

Keyboard shortcut

Open the popup with Ctrl + Shift + Y (or Command + Shift + Y on macOS). Change it on your browser's extension shortcuts page.

Privacy

Clipfox is deliberately boring about data: it stays inside your browser.

Local
  • No network requests, analytics, or remote code.
  • No host permissions. A page is read only after you press Scan this page.
  • Settings use chrome.storage.sync, so your browser account may sync them like other extension preferences.
  • Clipfox never downloads videos, touches cookies, or bypasses private or unlisted access.
